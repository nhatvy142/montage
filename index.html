    (About)

    Everything starts
    with a frame.

    Montage began with one idea: put the right images side by side and they start telling a story. It's our name — and still our method.

    Saigon is where we learned the craft. Loud, fast, never finished — the city taught us to move quickly and look twice.

    Our roots are in product, not just pictures. What we make has to survive the real world — a printed box, an app screen, a booth at CES.

    We stay curious about the strange stuff — astrology, old craft, game worlds. Each one is a reason to make the next frame better.
